**Falling in love with Quirk (at 1:30am)**

I've been stuck in tutorial hell with quantum computing for a while. I knew the gates, I knew how they worked on paper, but I had no real sense of where or how to use them.

That changed last weekend when I discovered [Quirk](https://algassert.com/quirk). Tonight, I've been playing with a fork of Quirk, a quantum circuit simulator created by [Craig Gidney](https://algassert.com/about.html), tweaking it to make it my own — you can try it here: [QubitBoard](https://rogersmukiibi.com/QubitBoard). Being able to actually _see_ the gates and play around with them instead of just reading about them has been incredibly satisfying. Something about the intuition finally clicking watered the seed of interest I've had in quantum computing for a long time.

I haven't gone deep yet, and I'm too tired tonight for a proper technical write-up. But I'll be spending a lot more time with this. Many thanks to Craig for building it. He has more tools that are currently above my pay grade :)

The future looks exciting.
