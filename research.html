A Smart IoT-enabled Multimodal System for Honeybee Keepers in Uganda

A. Turyagyenda, A. Katumba, R. Akol, M. Nsabagwa, M. E. Mkiramweni, R. Mukiibi

A field-deployed hive monitor combining temperature, humidity, weight, and vibration sensing. I designed the sensor PCB and the accelerometer pipeline that reads colony activity from FFTs of hive vibrations.

Unbiased AI Models for Solar Irradiance in Rural Africa

Dr J. Mikelson, Dr A. Katumba, R. Mukiibi, C. Nyakairu, N. W. Jenifer

Satellite estimates of solar irradiance run high over Uganda, and that bias flows straight into the cost of solar projects. We combine NASA POWER, CAMS, and MERRA-2 data with ground stations to correct it, and serve bias-corrected irradiance for any location and date in Sub-Saharan Africa.
