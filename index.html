Rogers Mukiibi

I'm an electrical engineer and Associate R&D Power Systems Engineer at Auri Energy, where I model distribution networks, study power quality, and help build digital twins of the grid. I studied Electrical Engineering at Makerere University.

Before that I was a research assistant at the Marconi Machine Learning Lab, building AI models to estimate solar irradiation across Uganda with GIZ and the Ministry of Energy, and an intern at the IoT-RA Lab, where I designed the hardware for a smart beehive monitor. These days I'm also working on quantum computing: applying QUBO and QAOA formulations to distribution network control, and building tools that make circuits easier to see.
